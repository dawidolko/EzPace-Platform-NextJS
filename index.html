How it works

  1. 1

    A conversation

    Twenty minutes about how much you run now, how much time you have in a week and when the goal is. Without that, a plan is guesswork.

  2. 2

    The plan

    You get a calendar with sessions, a pace for each and a heart-rate range. All of it counted back from race day, not appended to it.

  3. 3

    Correction

    Each week you send the data from your watch. If the pace is not landing, or a session falls through, next week takes that into account.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a heart-rate watch?

No. Without one we run the training by pace and perceived effort — the zones take longer to tune, but the plan works the same.

What if I miss a week?

Write to me and the next week gets rewritten. The plan is not a checklist; it is a proposal that has to fit your calendar.

Does the plan include a diet?

In the Marathon package — a fuelling strategy for the race itself and for long runs. We do not write full meal plans; that is a dietitian’s job.

How does contact with the coach work?

Messages on working days, answered within 24 hours. The Marathon package adds a call every two weeks.

Can I stop partway through?

Yes. We refund the proportional share for the unused weeks, no questions asked.

Start with a conversation

Twenty minutes about your goal and your calendar. No commitment — if the plan does not make sense in your timeframe, I will say so plainly.
